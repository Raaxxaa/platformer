# M7.1: Stage 2 — Both Roads — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The road chosen at the Crossroads inn leads somewhere:
- **Mountain road → Caves & Mountains** (`mountains`): more climbing. Bats, Goblin Miners, Rock Golems, Rams, the Goblin Shaman elite, and the **Stone Colossus**. Gimmicks: a long climb under falling rocks, ice peaks, a **minecart** ride, a cracked-wall skip.
- **Forest road → Dark Forest & Swamps** (`swamps`): more fighting. Bog Zombies, Giant Spiders, Wisps, Swamp Witches, the Treant elite, and the **Spider Queen**. Gimmicks: wading water, dark zones with fireflies, a grapple-vine skip.
- After the Guardian: the stat pick, then the **Border Village** (same layout as the Crossroads, its own art), whose inn leads to a "To be continued" screen until M9.

**Architecture:** Same as M3: ASCII chunks in `levels-src/<level>/` built by `tools/build_levels.py` into `public/levels/`, validated by `validateLevel`. New core systems for the surfaces and hazards; enemies on M2's AI layer; Guardians on M3's boss layer.

**Spec:**
- `docs/spec/06-run-levels-save.md` §8.2–8.5 (template, counts, Stage 2 road column, sizes and gimmicks per level), §9.4 (side arena: built but **empty** until the Dragon in M9), §10 (`falling_rocks`), §12.2 (Border Village), §13 (level format: `water`, `ice`, `cracked_wall`, `grapple_point`, `minecart`, `MinecartStop`, `dark_zone`, `firefly`).
- `docs/spec/01-movement.md` §13.2 (water), §13.3 (ice), §13.6 (minecart), §13.7 (grapple points).
- `docs/spec/04-enemies-bosses.md` §2 (shared rules, stage scaling ×1.4 HP for Stage 2), §4 (Caves & Mountains), §5 (Dark Forest & Swamps), §8 (their projectiles: `bomb`, `hex_bolt`, `potion`, …), §10.2 Stone Colossus, §10.3 Spider Queen (with Spiderlings).
- `docs/spec/05-loot-economy.md` §9.5 (exact enemy mix per level), §7.5 (Ambush packs for these biomes), §3 (Stage 2 odds rows, already data).
- `docs/spec/08-art-audio.md` §9.3 (water, ice tiles), §9.5 (rails), §10.2 (darkness).
- `docs/spec/03-classes-abilities.md` §9.2 (Shoulder Charge breaks cracked walls: fill in M4's `breakCrackedWalls()` hook).

## Level design (the builder writes the chunks from this)

Both levels follow the 8-section template (06 §8.2) with the Stage 2 road column: `mountains` 36 regulars (§2 10, §3 2, §4 6+2, §5 9, §6 3, §7 4), `swamps` 44 (§2 12, §3 4, §4 6+2, §5 9, §6 7, §7 4). Use the **exact per-type mix** of 05 §9.5 and spread each type across sections. Object counts 06 §8.4, including the **side arena** in §6 and a `dragon_warning` zone ≥ 640 px before it. Design rules from `docs/levels/plains.md` (teach then test, obvious main path, speed lines, fair placement) still apply; the Stage 1 spacing rule (06 §8.6) does not, but keep groups readable (max 3 regulars per group in §2).

### `mountains` (max 560 × 176 tiles: a tall level)

| § | What | Gimmick and enemies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mountain foot, loot stand | Calm. A sign hints that the road climbs |
| 2 | Rocky slopes | Goblin Miners throwing bombs from ledges, Rams on flat stretches near drops (their headbutt knocks you off), Bat Swarms in the open. Brute-equivalent elite: Goblin Shaman #1 shielding Miners |
| 3 | **The great climb** (≥ 60 tiles up) | Wall-jump shafts and ledges under `falling_rocks` zones; the 2 regulars are bats. Rest ledges every ~12 tiles. Lucky Block #2, Key #1 detour |
| 4 | Branch | Main path through a Rock Golem tunnel (hit them from above or behind); **skip route through a `cracked_wall`** (only Shoulder Charge breaks it; the main path never needs it). Side path: key chest, cursed chest, Shaman #2, ability stand |
| 5 | Mine arena | 3 waves (3, 3, 3 + Shaman #3), 20 × 18 tiles of mine floor with 2 platforms |
| 6 | **Ice peaks, then the minecart** | Icy ledges (ice tiles), the side arena (empty without the Dragon), Lucky Block #3, Key #2 after hard jumps, merchant spot, then a **minecart ride ≥ 120 tiles** downhill into the caves: jumps over gaps, low ceilings, a `MinecartStop` at the end |
| 7 | Crystal cave loot area | Key chests #2 and #3, timed door, Lucky Block #4, Shaman #4 |
| 8 | Colossus cavern + road | Boss arena (04 §9.2 size), road to `exit` |

### `swamps` (max 720 × 96 tiles: a long level)

| § | What | Gimmick and enemies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forest edge, loot stand | Calm, dusky |
| 2 | Bog | **Swamp water ≥ 30% of the floor** (wading slows you; dash and slide don't). Submerged Bog Zombies rising out of the water, Witches lobbing potions from dry mounds, Wisps. Treant #1 at the end |
| 3 | **Dark hollow** | `dark_zone` with fireflies lighting the path; hanging Giant Spiders over the path (they drop when you pass under), water pools. Lucky Block #2, Key #1 detour |
| 4 | Branch | Main path along the bog; **skip route on grapple vines** (`grapple_point`s; only Grapple Arrow uses them; the main path never needs them). Side path: key chest, cursed chest, Treant #2, ability stand |
| 5 | Sunken ruin arena | 3 waves (3, 3, 3 + Treant #3) |
| 6 | Deep swamp | Water and a second dark zone, the side arena (empty without the Dragon), Lucky Block #3, Key #2 after hard jumps, merchant spot (on dry ground, 15+ tiles) |
| 7 | Witch's grove loot area | Key chests #2 and #3, timed door, Lucky Block #4, Treant #4 |
| 8 | Queen's nest + road | Boss arena with a ceiling for her Ceiling Drop, road to `exit` |

## Global Constraints

- All earlier constraints hold. Level files are generated; same source → byte-identical JSON.
- Enemy HP and damage use Stage 2 scaling (00-README: mob HP ×1.4, boss HP ×1.7, damage ×1.3).
- **The main path never needs an ability**, a grapple point, a cracked wall or a dash-jump-only gap (06 §8.5).
- The Dragon is **not** in M7.1. The side arena is empty; the `dragon` roll still happens at run start (06 §4) so seeds stay stable, but a Dragon rolled for Stage 2 simply isn't spawned yet. Mark it `// SPEC-GAP: Dragon arrives in M9`; design chat's choice, no `SPEC-GAPS.md` entry.
